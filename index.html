Privacy, plainly.

Last updated November 2026
01

What we keep

Your Flame email address, the display name that comes from it, the words you put in a report, the location and type you choose, the time you posted, and any photo you attach.

We do not ask for a password, a phone number, or your live location. We do not keep a recording of your voice. A dictated transcript is sent to the refiner only when you use the voice note, and it is not stored unless you publish the report.

02

Who can see it

The feed is visible to anyone who has the board open. Reporter names appear on each card, but reporter email addresses do not appear in the public feed.

Email addresses are shown only in the contact dialog for signed-in students and on match cards. Photos are served from the same board and follow the same visibility.

03

Where it lives

Reports live in a SQLite database on the campus server. Photos are stored inside that database as compressed image data. The board does not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics, or advertising cookies.

Your browser stores your sign-in email in localStorage so you stay signed in on this device. Signing out removes that local value.

04

How long we keep it

A report stays on the board until the campus maintainer removes it or the database is reset. Signing out does not remove your reports.

If you want a report or photo removed, email the address below from your Flame account and include the item title. We will remove it as soon as we can.

Questions or removal

Talk to the board maintainer.

Write to lostfound@flame.edu.in. This is a small campus project, so replies may take a day or two.
